Our Work

Education Is The Foundation Of Sustainable Change

Education is one of the most effective tools for breaking cycles of poverty, ignorance and exclusion. Rivika Foundation’s education work is designed to improve access, learning quality and long-term continuity for children and young people from underserved communities.

Our approach combines education support with health, nutrition, digital learning, teacher development and community engagement so that children can learn, stay in school and progress with confidence.

Mission Education

What We Do

From foundational learning to mainstreaming and continued support, education initiatives are structured around the real needs of children and their communities.

01

Foundational & Bridge Education

Learning support for children who need a structured pathway into or back into formal education.

02

Remedial Learning

Additional support helps children strengthen core concepts, confidence and learning outcomes.

03

Digital & STEM Learning

Technology-enabled activities, computer exposure and experiential learning make education more relevant and engaging.

04

Teacher Development

Teachers are supported with training, learning resources and practical methodologies to improve classroom delivery.

05

Health & Nutrition Support

Health and nutrition are integrated because a child cannot learn effectively without wellbeing and care.

06

Community Engagement

Parents and local stakeholders are sensitised and engaged so that children can enter, stay and succeed in education.

Four-Pronged Approach

A holistic learning ecosystem

The programme model focuses on the child while also strengthening teachers, learning environments and community participation.

C

Children

Focus on learning outcomes, confidence, wellbeing and progression towards formal schooling.

T

Teachers

Training, support and resources to make teaching more effective and child-friendly.

L

Learning Environment

Safer, more engaging spaces with libraries, digital tools, activities and learning materials.

K

Community

Parents and local stakeholders participate in mobilisation, retention and sustained support.

Why Education Matters

Education is the key to a dignified future.

Relevant and quality education builds knowledge and skills, helps children make informed choices, strengthens social awareness and opens pathways to livelihoods and participation in society.

Our work therefore goes beyond putting a child into a classroom. It is about enabling children to learn effectively, remain engaged, access support and progress into mainstream opportunities.
